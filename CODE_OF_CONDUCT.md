# Sentra Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of the Sentra project, we are committed to making participation in our community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Standards of Behavior

### Positive Behaviors
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Providing and receiving constructive technical feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on safe, deterministic, and verifiable security research.
- Adhering to responsible and coordinated vulnerability disclosure practices.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, architectural preferences, and design approaches.

### Unacceptable Behaviors
- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information or unredacted confidential credentials without consent.
- Promoting weaponized exploits or unauthorized testing against third-party systems outside explicit scope.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source environment.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project maintainers via `taiizor@vegalya.com` or through GitHub repository management. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate or harmful.
