## Phase 1 Increment 5.2 — Domain models, session state machine, and policy engine

## Phase 1 Increment 5.6.2 — Core Service authorization, budget ordering & settlement reliability hardening

The [hardening report](docs/phase1/INCREMENT_5_6_2_CORE_SERVICE_SECURITY_HARDENING.md) details the targeted security and reliability remediations implemented in the Core Engine Service layer:

- **F-561-01 Remediation (Mandatory Policy Evaluation & Floor Enforcement)**: Enforced strict classification-floor assertion (`req.classification < session.classification_floor` returns `ServiceErrorKind::PolicyDenied`) at the execution dispatch boundary. Synchronized authoritative persistent session context into `PolicyEngine` via `PolicyEngine::register_session_context()` and `ApprovalBroker::set_session_context()`. Bound `CoreService::execute_task()` directly to `self.policy_engine.evaluate()`, preventing bypass of policy evaluations, unknown operations, and Bell-LaPadula information flow write-downs.
- **F-561-02 Remediation (Atomic Budget Reservation & Approval Consumption)**: Introduced `PrepareExecutionRequest` and atomic `reserve_budget_and_consume_approval()` on `PersistenceRepository`. Bundled in-transaction approval grant validation, budget limit checks, budget reservation insertion, and approval consumption update into a single SQLite write transaction (`manager.write(|tx| ...)`). If budget reservation fails due to `BudgetExceeded`, the entire transaction rolls back via SQLite WAL rollback; the approval grant remains `ISSUED` and unconsumed, preventing premature grant burning.
- **F-561-03 Remediation (Compound Error Propagation & Settlement Visibility)**: Eliminated silent discard of budget settlement errors in failure handlers. Extended `ServiceErrorKind` with compound variants `ExecutionWithSettlementFailure` and `EvidenceWithSettlementFailure`. Added `secondary_error` field, constructors, and predicates (`is_execution()`, `is_evidence()`, `has_settlement_failure()`, `secondary_error()`) to `ServiceError`. Unconfirmed reservations remain bounded and visible in persistence and audit logs.
- **Audit Consistency**: Both `BudgetReserved` and `ApprovalConsumed` audit events are emitted inside the single atomic SQLite transaction, guaranteeing that partial or orphan audit records are never committed.
- **Test Inventory & Validation**: Added 6 new integration tests in `crates/sentra-core/tests/service_tests.rs` (19 total service tests).
